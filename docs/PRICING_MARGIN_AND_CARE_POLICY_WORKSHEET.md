## Three-month measurement

Record all time: checks, troubleshooting, changes, client communication, testing, deployment and reporting. Give clients the monthly work summary and obtain written approval before exceeding agreed billable scope.

Allocate annual payments over 12 months when comparing monthly workload. Use a loaded internal hourly cost including compensation; the owner's time is not free.

## Calculate the floor

Required fee = (loaded labour + allocated tools/admin + unreimbursed provider costs + risk reserve) / (1 − target margin).

Choose a margin using actual costs. For example, a 35% margin means dividing costs by 0.65; that is illustrative, not an established margin or guarantee.

- Validate Essential against quarterly reviews, capped content changes and realistic support administration.
- Validate monthly plans at full usage, rather than assuming unused hours.
- Check that annual discounts leave adequate capacity and margin.
- Quote baseline technical care separately for CMS sites/applications without regular development needs.
- Include the cost of any first-year infrastructure allowance in the build fee; avoid unlimited hosting or maintenance promises.
- Keep negotiated discounts, margins and detailed estimating calculations in private business records.
